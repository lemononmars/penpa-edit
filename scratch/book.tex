\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

Welcome to the \textbf{Code Breaker Logic Puzzle Contest II Compendium}.

The Code Breaker contest series was founded to cultivate and celebrate the joy of pencil-and-paper logic puzzles in Southeast Asia. Combining globally beloved classic Nikoli genres with cutting-edge hybrid formats, the second edition features four championship competition rounds comprising \textbf{46 tournament puzzles}, alongside a comprehensive omnibus section \textbf{Choco Banana City Bus} featuring \textbf{25 handcrafted puzzles} across a broad spectrum of logic challenges:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Round 1 (Popular Picks)}: Fast-paced competition featuring the most played and celebrated genres from the logic puzzle community: \textit{Yajilin, Heyawake, LITS, Masyu, Akari,} and \textit{Shakashaka}.
    \item \textbf{Round 2 (Shading Showcase)}: A deep exploration of shaded-grid geometry, highlighting \textit{Nonogram, Snake, Nurikabe, Choco Banana,} and \textit{Aqre}.
    \item \textbf{Round 3 (Penta Party)}: A tightly interconnected metapuzzle round where six individual puzzle types (\textit{Slitherlink, Pipelink, Cave, Tapa, Fillomino, Spiral Galaxy}) each hide two distinct pentominoes, uniting into the complete 12-pentomino set that forms the solution to a grand \textit{Pentominous} grid.
    \item \textbf{Round 4 (Warped Words)}: An innovative Thai linguistic logic round featuring \textit{Word Search, Fill-in (Anagram), Scrabble,} and \textit{Cryptic Crossword} linked by a dynamic 3-word exchange mechanism between puzzles.
    \item \textbf{Special Section: Choco Banana City Bus}: A comprehensive omnibus tour of 25 handcrafted Choco Banana puzzles of increasing grid dimensions, from 7$\times$7 starter grids up to a 16$\times$16 master grid and a 5$\times$35 linear expressway.
    \item \textbf{Special Section: Logic Showcase}: A curated exhibition of innovative hybrid genres and experimental mechanics from the Logic Showcase series (LS 49--LS 75), featuring 47 unique handcrafted challenge grids.
\end{enumerate}

This volume serves as both the definitive record of the championship and a permanent reference textbook for solvers preparing for national and World Puzzle Championship (WPC) events.

\section*{Official Competition Regulations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Permitted Equipment}: Competitors may use pencils, non-red pens, highlighters, erasers, correction tape, rulers, scissors, blank scratch paper, a physical dictionary, and the official Instruction Booklet.
    \item \textbf{Prohibited Equipment}: All electronic devices (phones, smartwatches, calculators) must be powered off and stowed away from the competition table. Any audible alert may result in immediate round disqualification.
    \item \textbf{Submission \& Grading}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Rounds 1--3}: Full score (100\%) is awarded for exact matches with official keys. Minor mistakes (1--4 incorrect grid positions or line segments) receive partial credit of 80\%. Solutions with 5 or more discrepancies receive 0 points.
        \item \textbf{Round 4}: Each correctly placed word earns 1 point (local word) or 3 points (transferred word).
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Early Finish Time Bonus}: Solvers finishing before the round buzzer may submit early. If all puzzles are either completely correct or partially correct, an early finish bonus of \textbf{2 points per full minute remaining} is added to the round score.
    \item \textbf{Tie-Breaking Hierarchy}: Ties in overall score are resolved by comparing scores in Round 4, followed by Round 3, Round 2, and Round 1.
    \item \textbf{Credits}: Hosted at NK Board Game Seacon Bangkae. Puzzle editing and rendering powered by Penpa+.
\end{itemize}
